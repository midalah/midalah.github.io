AI-Assisted Marketing & Workflows

Using AI to accelerate the work without outsourcing the thinking.

My approach to AI combines human strategy, creative judgment, experimentation, and verification with tools that accelerate research, ideation, content development, visual exploration, and workflow execution.

OVERVIEW

AI is part of the process, not the strategy.

I use AI most effectively when it helps me move faster from question to insight, idea to iteration, or concept to execution.

The value does not come from accepting the first output. My workflow involves defining the problem, supplying context, evaluating results, refining prompts, challenging assumptions, and applying human judgment before anything becomes finished work.

This approach allows me to use AI across marketing and creative workflows while maintaining brand voice, audience relevance, accuracy, and strategic intent.

HOW I USE AI

Creative

Develop concepts, messaging directions, visual ideas, campaign themes, and creative variations.

Workflow

Accelerate repetitive tasks, structure information, refine drafts, and improve the path from idea to execution.

02 · PROMPT & CONTEXT DESIGN

Better context creates better output.

Prompting Iteration Context Design Quality Control

What Changed My Output

The biggest improvement in my AI workflow came from moving away from one-line requests and toward structured context.

I define the audience, objective, constraints, brand context, desired outcome, and relevant background before asking for an output.

Iteration Matters

I rarely treat the first generation as finished. I refine the brief, challenge weak assumptions, compare alternatives, narrow the direction, and edit the final result.

MY AI PRINCIPLES

Faster does not mean careless.

01

Start With the Problem

Use AI because it improves a workflow, not because AI needs to be inserted into everything.

02

Provide Context

Better inputs include audience, objective, constraints, relevant history, and desired outcomes.

03

Question the Output

Treat generated responses as material to evaluate, not automatic truth.

04

Protect the Brand

Maintain consistent voice, standards, messaging, and creative judgment.

05

Verify

Check facts, claims, assumptions, and important information before relying on them.

06

Keep Humans Accountable

The person using the tool remains responsible for the decision and the finished work.
