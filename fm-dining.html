F&M Diplomat Dining

Integrated Marketing & Campus Engagement

Developing campaigns, experiences, and brand initiatives that connected dining with student life at Franklin & Marshall College.

OVERVIEW

More than dining promotion.

My marketing work for F&M Diplomat Dining combined campaign strategy, social media, experiential marketing, content creation, community partnerships, and brand development.

I developed initiatives designed to increase student engagement, promote dining programs and events, strengthen the dining brand, and create experiences that connected campus dining with the broader Franklin & Marshall community.

The work extended beyond digital promotion. Campaigns often moved from concept and creative development into physical campus activations, student participation, partnerships, and measurable outcomes.

Stop Hunger Dunk Tank

Experiential Marketing Social Media Community Partnership

The Strategy

02 · BRAND ENGAGEMENT

Building Ele Winks

Social Strategy Community-Led Branding Audience Engagement

The Idea

Create a recognizable personality for Diplomat Dining that students could interact with across both social media and the physical campus experience.

The Strategy

Instead of choosing a mascot internally, I developed a participatory campaign that invited students to help build the brand.

Students first voted on the type of mascot. After the elephant was selected, they submitted name ideas and participated in another round of voting to select the final name: Ele Winks.
